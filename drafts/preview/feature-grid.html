What 0.6.6 proves

Digest-bound plans

Every plan snapshots the exact actions and expires — no stale plan can be replayed against a moved target.

Explicit approval

Commit requires the human to name the exact mutating action ids, not just click "yes."

Read-only by default

Inspection and freshness checks never touch admin, preview, or live APIs.

Printed receipts

Every boundary crossing — plan, execute, verify — produces a receipt: what ran, what changed, what refused.

Contract-checked content

Section and media URL strictness catch malformed authoring before it ships.

Defensive check: title cell present-but-empty, description-only row still renders in place.
